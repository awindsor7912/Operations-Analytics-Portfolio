Metric Glossary

Example reporting definitions.

Metric clarity improves dashboard credibility because reviewers can understand what each visual is intended to control.

Metric Definition Operational Use
Unbilled Revenue Estimated or completed work that has not yet progressed through billing completion. Identifies revenue exposure and backlog risk.
Adjustment Volume Count of invoice adjustment records in the selected reporting period. Monitors frequency of billing corrections or rework.
Adjustment Amount Total financial impact associated with invoice adjustment records. Supports financial review and variance analysis.
Aging Bucket Grouped time interval showing how long work has remained open, unbilled, or unresolved. Prioritizes overdue or high-risk items.
Exception Rate Percentage of records requiring manual review, correction, approval, or follow-up. Measures process quality and control effectiveness.
